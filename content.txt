Omnicorum (Nico Russo)
21 years old.
Raised in Connecticut USA, going to school at the University of Miami in Florida.
Makes heavy yet melodiccally driven bass music (dubstep, trap, colour bass, house)
Got started doing video game remixes in LMMS (Linux Multi-Media Software), moved to
FL Studio and started creating original music, then moved to Ableton Live.
Influenced by artists like Space Laces, Virtual Riot, Skrillex, Sharks, and more.
Highlights: The End of an Era ARG. modus Superluminal Remixed contest winner, remixed the song Superluminal by modus.
