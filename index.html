About

Omnicorum is the bass music project of Nico Russo, blending heavy sound design with melodic hooks across dubstep, trap, colour bass and house. Raised in Connecticut and now studying Music Engineering Technology at the University of Miami's Frost School of Music, he won modus's Superluminal remix contest and released The End of an Era EP on Neoluminum in 2026, launched alongside an alternate reality game.
