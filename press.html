They were a winner of modus’s “Superluminal” remix contest in 2023 and has had tracks featured on compilations including Exobolt Worlds Vol. 2, All Nighter, Vol. 9 on FORM, and Mothership Vol. 2. Full bio

Omnicorum is the bass music project of Nico Russo, a producer from Connecticut. Drawing on dubstep, trap, colour bass and house, their tracks set dense, heavily designed low end against melodic leads and chord work.

Russo started producing by remixing video game music in LMMS, the free open-source music software, before moving to FL Studio to write original material and later to Ableton Live, where they work today. They cite Space Laces, Virtual Riot, Skrillex and Sharks among their influences. Alongside producing, they study Music Engineering Technology at the University of Miami’s Frost School of Music, with minors in both computer engineering and computer science.

In 2023, Russo was a winner of modus’s remix contest for “Superluminal,” and the remix was released on Superluminal Remixed. Their tracks have since appeared on several compilations, including “Paradises” on Mothership Vol. 2, “Coconut Mist” on All Nighter, Vol. 9 on FORM, and “Hyper Thug Market” on Exobolt Worlds Vol. 2. They released Digitalis on Neoluminum in March 2024.

Their latest EP The End of an Era followed on Neoluminum on October 2, 2026. Ahead of the release, Russo built an alternate reality game into omnicorum.com: a hidden archive of audio, text and image puzzles that led players to the EP and a free pack of sounds taken from it.
